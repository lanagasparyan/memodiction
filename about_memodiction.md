## What is MemoDiction?
MemoDiction is a dictionary that helps you remember words better. It is a collection of words that are commonly used in the English language, along with their definitions, synonyms, antonyms, examples in sentences, mnemonics for remembering, etymology and history, idiomatic expressions/phrases, regional variations (if applicable), and common misconceptions/errors.

## Why MemoDiction?

Remembering words is hard. It's even harder to remember words that you don't use often. We believe that the second best way to remember words is through mnemonics. Also, it is hard to understand the nuances of a word without knowing its etymology and history. MemoDiction aims to solve these problems by providing a comprehensive list of words along with their mnemonics, etymology and history, and more.
This can help you not only prepare for exams like the SAT, GRE, and GMAT, but also improve your vocabulary in general.

## How to use MemoDiction?
For now you can use the search bar to search for words. We are working on adding more features to the website, so stay tuned!

## How can I add words to MemoDiction?
You can add words to MemoDiction by filling out [this form](https://forms.gle/VX2hUtLnV87UDyvv8). We will review the words and add them to the website if they meet our criteria. If the word gets added and you have filled out your name in the form, we will add your name to the word's page as a contributor. Thank you for your contribution!
In case you want to add words in bulk, please contact us at `lana5545 at gmail dot com`

## How can I contribute to mnemonics for words?
We use Disqus for comments on the website. You can add your mnemonics as comments on the word's page. If we found that the mnemonics is not appropriate, we will remove it.
