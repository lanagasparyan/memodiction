    synonyms:
    - Wood shavings
    - Wood dust
    antonyms:
    - N/a
    examples:
    - The carpenter swept up the sawdust from the workshop floor.

  etymology:
    origin: The word "sawdust" originated from the combination of "saw" and "dust."
    historical_usage: The term has been commonly used since ancient times, as woodworking
      has been practiced for centuries.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Sawdust in the air"'
    explanation: This phrase refers to the presence and abundance of sawdust particles
      in the atmosphere, typically indicating ongoing woodworking or carpentry activities.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Considering sawdust as fine sand-like material.
    explanation: Sawdust consists of small wood particles, not grains or minerals
