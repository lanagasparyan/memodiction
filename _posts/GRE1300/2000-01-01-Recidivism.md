      gained prominence in criminology and legal discourse.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Cycle of recidivism"'
    explanation: Refers to the pattern or repetitive nature of relapsing into criminal

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "recidivism" with "rehabilitation."
