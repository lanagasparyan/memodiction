  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing a poncho with a cape or cloak.
    explanation: While ponchos, capes, and cloaks are similar in that they are all
      outer garments worn over the shoulders, they have distinct characteristics.

      piece of fabric, while capes and cloaks may have additional fastenings or multiple
