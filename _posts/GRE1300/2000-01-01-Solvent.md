  - part_of_speech: Noun

    synonyms:
    - Dissolvent
    - Dissolver
    - Diluent
    antonyms:
    - Insoluble
    - Undissolved

    mnemonics:
    - Think of solvent as something that solves the problem of dissolving other substances.
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Having the power to dissolve or capable of dissolving another substance.
    synonyms:
    - Dissolving
    - Soluble
    - Soluble in
    antonyms:
    - Insoluble
    - Undissolvable
    - Undissolved
    examples:
    - This solvent solution can effectively remove tough stains.
    - The solvent properties of this chemical make it ideal for cleaning grease.
    mnemonics:
    - Remember that a solvent is a solution-maker.
  etymology:
    origin: 'Late Middle English (as an adjective): from Latin solvent- ''loosening'',

    historical_usage: The word has been used in chemistry and scientific contexts
      since ancient times.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Financially solvent"'
    explanation: Refers to a person, company, or organization that is able to pay
      their debts and obligations.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing solvent (noun) with solute (a substance dissolved in

    explanation: Solvent is the substance that does the dissolving, while solute is
