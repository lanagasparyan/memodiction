data:
  word: Gerontocracy
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - A system of government in which old people exercise the dominant power or control.
    synonyms:
    - Rule by the elderly
    - Aging leadership
    antonyms:
    - Youthocracy
    - Rule by the young
    examples:
    - The country's gerontocracy prioritized policies that favored the older population.
    - Critics argue that gerontocracy hinders progress and innovation within a society.
    mnemonics:
    - '''Geront'' can be associated with the root ''geron'', which means old age.'
  etymology:
    origin: The word "gerontocracy" is derived from the Greek words "geron" (old man)
      and "kratos" (rule, power).
    historical_usage: The term first appeared in English in the early 19th century
      and was used in discussions about various forms of government.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Power of the gray"'
    explanation: A phrase used to describe the political influence and control exerted
      by the elderly population in a gerontocracy.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Gerontocracy only refers to a government exclusively led by old
      people.
    explanation: While gerontocracy implies the dominance of old age in government,
      it does not necessarily mean exclusive rule by the elderly. It can coexist with
      other age groups in positions of power.
