  - part_of_speech: Verb

    - Pretend
    - Bluff
    - Simulate
    antonyms:
    - Reveal
    - Expose
    examples:
    - The football player feinted to the right and then quickly changed direction.

    origin: From late Middle English 'feinte', from Old French 'feint', past participle

    historical_usage: The term "feint" originates from medieval warfare tactics, where

  idioms:

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "feint" with "faint"
