  word: Voluptuous
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Suggesting or characterized by luxury or sensual pleasure; full or curvaceous

    synonyms:
    - Sensual
    - Seductive
    - Alluring
    antonyms:
    - Unattractive
    - Unappealing
    - Repulsive

    - Think of the word "voluptuous" as describing something that is visually appealing

    - The curves of the letter "V" in "voluptuous" represent the curves associated

  etymology:
    origin: From the Latin word "voluptuosus" meaning "full of pleasure." It is derived
      from the Latin word "voluptas" meaning "pleasure" or "delight."
    historical_usage: The term "voluptuous" has been used to describe aesthetically
      pleasing and sexually attractive qualities in art, literature, and descriptions
      of physical beauty throughout history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Voluptuous figure"'
    explanation: Referring to a seductive and curvaceous body shape, often associated
      with attractiveness and sensuality.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Using "voluptuous" solely to describe physical beauty.
    explanation: While "voluptuous" can be used in reference to physical attractiveness,

      beyond just appearances.
