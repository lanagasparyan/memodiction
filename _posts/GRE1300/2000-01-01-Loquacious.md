    origin: The word "loquacious" comes from the Latin word "loquax" meaning talkative.

  idioms:
  - expression: '"Birds of a feather flock together."'

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "loquacious" with "eloquent."
    explanation: While both words involve the use of words and speaking, "loquacious"

      refers to the ability to speak or write fluently, persuasively, and effectively.
