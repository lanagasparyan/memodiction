  word: Abide

  - part_of_speech: Verb

    - To remain or continue in a specified condition or position.
    synonyms:

    - Comply
    - Obey
    - Endure
    - Tolerate

    - Defy
    - Disobey
    - Resist
    examples:

    - He chose to abide by his decision and refused to change his mind.

    - '"A-Bide" can be remembered as "Accept and Observe".'

  etymology:
    origin: Old English 'abīdan', from 'ā-' (expressing completion) + 'bīdan' (to
      wait, remain).
    historical_usage: The word has been used since Old English and has maintained
      its core meaning of waiting or remaining, but with additional senses that have
      developed over time.

  - expression: '"Abide by"'

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'abide' with 'abode' or 'abounded'.
