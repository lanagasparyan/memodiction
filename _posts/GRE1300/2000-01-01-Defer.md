    historical_usage: The word "defer" has been in use since the late 14th century
      with its meaning centered around postponing or delaying actions or decisions.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Defer to someone"'

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "defer" with "differ".
