data:
  word: Accolade
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - An award or honor given to someone in recognition of their achievement or contribution.
    - Praise or approval given to someone for their accomplishments.
    synonyms:
    - Award
    - Honor
    - Commendation
    antonyms:
    - Criticism
    - Disapproval
    - Reprimand
    examples:
    - She received several accolades for her groundbreaking research.
    - The film was met with critical acclaim and received numerous accolades.
    mnemonics:
    - An accolade is like a pat on the back, acknowledging someone's success.
    - Think of accolades as a form of applause for exceptional achievement.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "accolade" comes from the French term "accolader," meaning "embrace"
      or "embrace as a mark of honor." It entered the English language in the 17th
      century.
    historical_usage: Originally, the term referred to an embrace or the act of knighting
      someone by tapping their shoulder with a sword. Over time, it evolved to represent
      any form of recognition or praise given to someone.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Bestow accolades"'
    explanation: The phrase means to give awards or recognition to deserving individuals
      or organizations.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Accolades are limited to awards only.
    explanation: While accolades can refer to specific awards, they can also encompass
      general praise or approval for someone's achievements.
