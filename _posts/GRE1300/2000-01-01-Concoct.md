  etymology:
    origin: From Latin 'concoctus', past participle of 'concoquere' meaning "to cook,
      digest".

      initially referred to cooking or digesting food, but later expanded to include
      the figurative sense of inventing or creating.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Concoct a plan"'
    explanation: Refers to creating or devising a plan, often with a clever or cunning
      approach.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'concoct' with 'cocktail' or 'mix'.
