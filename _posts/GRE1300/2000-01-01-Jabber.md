    - Be silent
    - Keep quiet

    origin: Late Middle English via Middle Low German 'jabbern', of imitative origin.
    historical_usage: The term has been used since the late Middle Ages to describe
      rapid, unintelligible speech.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Jabberwocky"'
    explanation: This is a nonsense poem written by Lewis Carroll in his novel "Through
      the Looking-Glass." The term has come to represent language or speech that is
      incomprehensible or meaningless.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Considering 'jabber' to have a negative connotation.
