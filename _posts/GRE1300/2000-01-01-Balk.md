categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_1, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Balk
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To hesitate or be unwilling to accept an idea, challenge, or task.
    - To obstruct or impede progress or movement.
    synonyms:
    - Hesitate
    - Resist
    - Block
    - Hinder
    antonyms:
    - Accept
    - Comply
    - Facilitate
    - Assist
    examples:
    - She balked at the idea of jumping off the cliff.
    - The fallen tree across the road balked their way.
    mnemonics:
    - Imagine a horse that refuses to move forward and stops abruptly (similar to
      hesitating or resisting).
  etymology:
    origin: Early 17th century from the obsolete adverbial use of balk, meaning "a
      bank for winning games" in the game of bowls. It later came to mean "an unplowed
      strip of land".
    historical_usage: Initially used in the game of bowls, then expanded to describe
      obstacles or refusal in various contexts.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Balk at"'
    explanation: To show reluctance or hesitation towards something.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'balk' with 'bawl'.
    explanation: '''Balk'' and ''bawl'' are homophones but have different meanings.
      ''Bawl'' means to cry loudly or shout.'
