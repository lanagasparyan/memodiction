    - Awkward

    - Unrefined

    - Graceful
    - Elegant

  etymology:
    origin: French; literally means "left"
    historical_usage: The term "gauche" originated from French in the 15th century,
      and it initially meant "left" as opposed to "right." Over time, it evolved to
      also mean "awkward" or "clumsy," describing a lack of skill or finesse, similar
      to a person who is left-handed struggling to use their right hand.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Bull in a china shop"'
    explanation: This expression is often used to describe someone who is clumsy or
      lacking finesse, similar to being gauche. It emphasizes the idea of someone

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "gauche" with "gaucho."
