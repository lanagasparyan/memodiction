data:
  word: Predilection
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - A special liking or preference for something; a mental inclination or bias.
    synonyms:
    - Preference
    - Inclination
    - Penchant
    antonyms:
    - Aversion
    - Dislike
    - Indifference
    examples:
    - She has a predilection for spicy food.
    - His predilection for classical music is evident in his extensive collection.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Predisposed to a particular selection"'
  etymology:
    origin: The word 'predilection' comes from the Latin word 'prae-' meaning "before"
      or "beyond" and 'dilectio' meaning "choice" or "love."
    historical_usage: The term has been in use since the 17th century to describe
      a strong liking or preference for something.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Predilection for the arts"'
    explanation: Refers to a strong preference or fondness for activities or creations
      in the artistic field, such as painting, music, or literature.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'predilection' with 'predestination'.
    explanation: '''Predilection'' refers to a personal preference, while ''predestination''
      refers to the belief in fate or a predetermined outcome. They have different
      meanings and contexts.'
