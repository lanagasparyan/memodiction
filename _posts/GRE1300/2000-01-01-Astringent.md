  word: Astringent

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Sharp
    - Biting
    - Tart

    - Sweet

    historical_usage: The term "astringent" has been used in medicine and skincare

      century.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Astringent taste"'
    explanation: Referring to the sensation of sharpness or bitterness that causes

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
