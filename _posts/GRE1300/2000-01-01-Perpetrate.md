  word: Perpetrate

  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To carry out or commit (a harmful, illegal, or immoral action, especially a
      crime).
    synonyms:
    - Commit
    - Carry out
    - Execute
    antonyms:
    - Prevent
    - Stop
    - Deter
    examples:
    - The criminals planned to perpetrate a robbery.
    - He was accused of perpetrating a fraud scheme.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of the word "perpetrate" as "perpetrating" or "persistently carrying out"
      a harmful action.
  etymology:
    origin: From Latin "perpetratus", past participle of "perpetrare" meaning "to
      accomplish, perform"
    historical_usage: The word "perpetrate" has been in use since the 16th century
      and has retained its meaning of committing a harmful action.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Perpetrate a crime"'
    explanation: Referring to committing or carrying out a criminal act.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "perpetrate" with "perpetuate".
    explanation: '"Perpetrate" means to commit or carry out an action, usually a harmful
      one, while "perpetuate" means to prolong or sustain something, often used in
      the context of keeping something negative alive.'
