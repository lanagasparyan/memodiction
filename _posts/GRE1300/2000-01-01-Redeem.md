      a similar meaning throughout history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Redeem oneself"'
    explanation: To do something good or helpful in order to improve one's own reputation

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "redeem" with "revenge".
