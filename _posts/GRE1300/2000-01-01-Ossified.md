  word: Ossified

  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Turned to bone; hardened like bone.
    synonyms:
    - Calcified
    - Petrified
    - Solidified
    antonyms:
    - Flexible
    - Supple
    - Pliable
    examples:
    - The ossified remains of the ancient dinosaur were discovered in the rock formation.
    - His ossified beliefs prevented him from accepting any new ideas.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of ossified as "turned to stone" or "hardened like bone".
    - Imagine someone being unable to bend due to their ossified joints.
  etymology:
    origin: Late 18th century from French 'ossifier', from Latin 'os, oss' (bone)
      + 'facere' (to make).
    historical_usage: The term evolved from its Latin roots to describe the process
      of turning into bone or becoming hardened.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Ossified thinking"'
    explanation: Refers to rigid or inflexible thoughts, beliefs, or opinions.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'ossified' with 'fossilized'.
    explanation: Although both words can refer to hardening or becoming more rigid,
      'ossified' specifically relates to the process of turning into bone or becoming
      bone-like, while 'fossilized' refers to the process of becoming a fossil.
