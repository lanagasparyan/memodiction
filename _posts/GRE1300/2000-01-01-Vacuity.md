    - Emptiness
    - Void
    - Nothingness
    antonyms:
    - Fullness
    - Meaning
    - Substance

      empty.
    historical_usage: The word has been used in English since the late 16th century.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Vacuum of vacuity"'
    explanation: Emphasizes the lack of substance, intelligence, or meaning in a particular

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'vacuity' with 'vacation' due to similar sound.
