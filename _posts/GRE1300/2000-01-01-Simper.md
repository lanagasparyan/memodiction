    origin: 'Late Middle English (as a verb): perhaps from the verb ''simper'', imitative

    historical_usage: The word has been used to describe a silly, affected smile since
      its origin.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Simper and bow"'

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Simper means a genuine, pleasant smile.
