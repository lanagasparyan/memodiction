      consistent throughout its history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Countervailing duties"'
    explanation: Refers to import duties imposed on imported goods in order to offset

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "countervail" with "counterfeit."
