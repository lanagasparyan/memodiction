  word: Officious

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Nosy
    - Overbearing
    antonyms:
    - Unobtrusive
    - Reserved
    - Indifferent

    origin: The word "officious" originated from the Latin word "officiosus," which

    historical_usage: The term has been in use since the early 16th century, with
      its original meaning centered around the performance of official duties. Over
      time, it acquired the negative connotation of being overly intrusive or overbearing.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Officious bystander"'
    explanation: Refers to someone who interferes or involuntarily inserts themselves

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "officious" with "official."
