data:
  word: Atonement
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - The act of making amends or reparation for a wrongdoing or sin; the reconciliation
      of God and humanity through the sacrificial death of Jesus Christ.
    synonyms:
    - Reparation
    - Expiation
    - Redemption
    antonyms:
    - Condemnation
    - Punishment
    - Guilt
    examples:
    - The criminal sought atonement for his crimes by donating to charity.
    - The concept of atonement is central to Christian theology.
    mnemonics:
    - Atonement refers to making up for wrongdoings or sins.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English 'at-one-ment', from atone (to reconcile) + -ment (suffix
      indicating action or result).
    historical_usage: The word originated in the 16th century and is primarily associated
      with Christian theology, specifically the reconciliation of humanity with God
      through the sacrifice of Jesus Christ.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Day of Atonement"'
    explanation: The holiest and most solemn day in the Jewish calendar, Yom Kippur,
      when sins are atoned for through prayer, fasting, and repentance.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Atonement only refers to the death of Jesus.
    explanation: While the death of Jesus is a central aspect of atonement in Christian
      theology, the concept encompasses the broader idea of making amends or reparation
      for wrongdoing or sin.
