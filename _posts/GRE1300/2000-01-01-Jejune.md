data:
  word: Jejune
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Lacking interest or significance; dull; banal.
    - Immature; childish.
    synonyms:
    - Boring
    - Vapid
    - Insipid
    antonyms:
    - Interesting
    - Stimulating
    - Mature
    examples:
    - The movie was criticized for its jejune and predictable plot.
    - His jejune understanding of the topic was evident through his simplistic arguments.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of a "juvenile" and "uneventful" situation as being jejune.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "jejune" comes from the Latin word "jejunus," meaning "fasting"
      or "hungry."
    historical_usage: The word first appeared in English in the 17th century, primarily
      being used to describe a lack of substance, depth, or maturity.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Jejune taste"'
    explanation: Referring to someone's lack of sophistication or discernment in matters
      of taste.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "jejune" with "juvenile" due to their similar sounds.
    explanation: While "jejune" can be used to describe something immature or childish,
      it goes beyond just being youthful and also signifies a lack of significance
      or dullness.
