      applied to physical or geographical wandering. Over time, it evolved to include
      the sense of verbal or written expression with a focus on providing extensive
      information.

  - expression: '"Expatiate upon"'
    explanation: Used to indicate going into great detail or elaboration on a particular
      subject.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
