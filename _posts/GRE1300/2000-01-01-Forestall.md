      a similar meaning throughout its history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Forestall someone''s plans"'
    explanation: Refers to taking action in advance to prevent someone else's plans

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "forestall" with "foresee".
