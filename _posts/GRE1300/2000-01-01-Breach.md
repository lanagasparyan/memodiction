  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Violation
    - Infraction
    - Transgression

    - Compliance

    - Adherence

      physical barrier.'
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - Make a gap in and break through (a wall, barrier, or defense).
    - Break or fail to observe (a law, agreement, or code of conduct).
    synonyms:
    - Violate
    - Infringe
    - Transgress
    antonyms:
    - Uphold
    - Observe
    - Comply
    examples:
    - The intruders breached the security system.
    - The employee breached the confidentiality agreement.
    mnemonics:
    - Picture someone breaking through a wall, symbolizing breaching.
  etymology:
    origin: Old English 'brecan', of Germanic origin; related to Dutch 'breken' and

    historical_usage: The word has maintained its meaning of breaking or failing to

  idioms:
  - expression: '"Breach of trust"'

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
