data:
  word: Preternatural
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Beyond what is normal or natural; abnormal.
    synonyms:
    - Supernatural
    - Eerie
    - Uncanny
    antonyms:
    - Natural
    - Ordinary
    - Normal
    examples:
    - The magician's ability to levitate objects seemed preternatural.
    - The cat's preternatural hearing allowed it to detect the slightest noise.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "pre-" as meaning "before" or "beyond," and "natural" as referring
      to what is normal. Preternatural, therefore, describes something beyond what
      is normal.
  etymology:
    origin: The word originated in the early 17th century from the Latin "praeternaturalis,"
      which combines "praeter" meaning "beyond" or "besides," and "naturalis" meaning
      "natural."
    historical_usage: Preternatural has been used to describe phenomena or abilities
      that were considered beyond the realm of nature or ordinary experience.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Preternatural calm"'
    explanation: Refers to an unusually calm or composed state, often in tense or
      chaotic situations.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing preternatural with supernatural.
    explanation: While preternatural and supernatural are similar in meaning, preternatural
      refers to something beyond what is normal or natural, while supernatural refers
      to something attributed to forces beyond scientific understanding. It is important
      to distinguish between the two terms and use them correctly.
