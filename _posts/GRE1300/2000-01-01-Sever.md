data:
  word: Sever
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To divide by cutting or slicing, especially suddenly and forcibly; separate.
    - To break off or end a relationship, connection, or association.
    synonyms:
    - Separate
    - Divide
    - Cut off
    - Detach
    antonyms:
    - Connect
    - Join
    - Unite
    examples:
    - The surgeon had to sever the artery to prevent further bleeding.
    - She decided to sever all ties with her toxic friends.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of a sharp knife severing a rope.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English 'severen', from Old French 'sevrer', from Latin 'separare'
      (to separate)
    historical_usage: The word 'sever' has been in use since Middle English and has
      always indicated the act of dividing or cutting.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Severance pay"'
    explanation: A sum of money paid by an employer to terminated employees as compensation
      for the loss of their job.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'sever' with 'severe'.
    explanation: '''Sever'' refers to an action of cutting or separating, while ''severe''
      describes something that is intense, harsh, or serious. These words have different
      meanings and should not be used interchangeably.'
