  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Barbarian

  etymology:
    origin: The word "Philistine" originally referred to the Philistines, an ancient
      people who lived in the region of Philistia (present-day Palestine) during biblical
      times. The Hebrew Bible portrays the Philistines as enemies of the Israelites
      and as a people characterized by their lack of cultural sophistication. Over
      time, the term "Philistine" developed a metaphorical usage to describe someone
      who is insensitive or contemptuous toward art, culture, and intellectual pursuits.
    historical_usage: The derogatory sense of "Philistine" emerged in the early 19th
      century and gained prominence through its association with artistic and intellectual
      circles.

  misconceptions:

      lack of appreciation for art, culture, and intellectual pursuits.
    explanation: It is important to use the term "Philistine" accurately to preserve
