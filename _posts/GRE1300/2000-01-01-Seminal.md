  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Influential

    - Pioneering
    - Formative
    antonyms:
    - Unremarkable
    - Insignificant

      or ideas.
  etymology:
    origin: From Late Latin "seminalis," from Latin "semen" meaning seed or source.
    historical_usage: The word has been used in English since the late 14th century
      with its original meaning of relating to semen or seed. The figurative meaning
      of strongly influencing later developments emerged later.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Seminal work/study/book"'
    explanation: Refers to a work or study that has had a significant impact on a
      particular field or subject.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "seminal" with "substantial" or "important."
    explanation: While seminal work or ideas may be substantial or important, the
