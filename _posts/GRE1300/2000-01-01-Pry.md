    origin: Early 19th century, from Scottish dialect, related to earlier Scots and

    historical_usage: The word "pry" has been in use since the early 19th century,
      primarily to describe the act of prying into someone's private affairs or forcing
      something open.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Pry into"'
    explanation: This phrase refers to investigating or trying to find out about someone's
      secret or personal matters without their permission.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
