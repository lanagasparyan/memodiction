    origin: The word "Epicurean" comes from the name of the ancient Greek philosopher

      The Latin adjective "epicureus" derived from the Greek "epikoureos" meaning
      "follower of Epicurus."
    historical_usage: Epicurus' philosophy and his emphasis on seeking pleasure and
      avoiding pain influenced the development of Epicureanism, a school of thought
      in ancient Greece. The term "Epicurean" has been used since the 16th century
      to describe those who pursue a life of sensual enjoyment and pleasure.
