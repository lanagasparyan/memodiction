  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Hostility
    - Animosity
    - Antagonism
    antonyms:
    - Friendship

    - Harmony
    examples:

    origin: 'Middle English: from Old French ''enemitié'', based on Latin ''inimicitia'',
      from ''inimicus'' (hostile).'
    historical_usage: The word has been used in English since the 14th century to
      describe a state of being an enemy or displaying hostility.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Bitter enmity"'
    explanation: Refers to a strong and intense hostility or animosity.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing enmity with animosity.
    explanation: While enmity and animosity are similar in meaning, enmity specifically
