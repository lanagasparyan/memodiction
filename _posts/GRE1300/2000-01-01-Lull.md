  word: Lull

  - part_of_speech: Verb

    - Calm
    - Pacify
    - Still
    antonyms:
    - Agitate
    - Disturb

    - Think of a lullaby that puts you or someone else to sleep.
    - Imagine the sound of a lullaby quietly calming a restless person.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English lullen, perhaps imitative of a lulling sound.
    historical_usage: The word "lull" has been used since Middle English times, and
      its origin suggests it may be imitative of the sound it describes.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Lull before the storm"'
    explanation: Refers to a period of calm or quiet before a potentially chaotic
      or turbulent event.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "lull" with "loll".
