data:
  word: Peregrination
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - The act of traveling or wandering about from place to place.
    synonyms:
    - Journey
    - Trip
    - Expedition
    antonyms:
    - Stay
    - Settlement
    - Residence
    examples:
    - After retiring, she embarked on a peregrination around the world.
    - His peregrination through Europe enlightened him about different cultures.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "peregrine falcon," a bird known for its long-distance migration.
  etymology:
    origin: From the Latin word "peregrinatio" meaning "foreign travel" or "journey
      abroad".
    historical_usage: The term has been used since the 16th century to refer to both
      physical and metaphorical journeys.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Peregrinations of the mind"'
    explanation: Refers to mental or intellectual wanderings or explorations.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Conflating peregrination with permanent settlement.
    explanation: Peregrination implies movement and temporary stays, while settlement
      suggests a fixed location.
