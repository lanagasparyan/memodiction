data:
  word: Caustic
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Able to burn or corrode organic tissue by chemical action.
    synonyms:
    - Corrosive
    - Burning
    - Acidic
    - Mordant
    antonyms:
    - Gentle
    - Mild
    - Soothing
    examples:
    - The caustic liquid burned through the metal surface.
    - He couldn't stand the caustic remarks about his appearance.
    mnemonics:
    - '''Caustic'' sounds like ''causes tick'' - the liquid causes a burning sensation
      like a tickle.'
  etymology:
    origin: Late 14th century from Latin 'causticus' and Greek 'kaustikos', from 'kaustos'
      meaning "burnt," from 'kaiein' meaning "to burn."
    historical_usage: The word has been used since the Late Middle English period
      and has primarily referred to the ability to burn or corrode.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Caustic wit"'
    explanation: Referring to a sharp, sarcastic, or cutting sense of humor that can
      burn or sting.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'caustic' with 'caustic soda.'
    explanation: Caustic soda, also known as sodium hydroxide, is a commonly known
      chemical compound, but it is just one example of a caustic substance. The term
      'caustic' is not limited to caustic soda and can be used to describe any substance
      with the ability to burn or corrode organic tissue.
