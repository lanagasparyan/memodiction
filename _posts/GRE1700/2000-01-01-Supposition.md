  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Certainty

    - Knowledge

  etymology:
    origin: From Latin "suppositio", from "supponere" (to put under, assume)

      century, originally derived from Latin.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"In supposition"'
    explanation: Refers to the state of assuming or hypothesizing something without
      certainty.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "supposition" with "fact" or "knowledge".
