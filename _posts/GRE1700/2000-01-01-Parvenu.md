  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Aristocrat

      used to describe individuals who have risen to a higher social status through

  idioms:
  - expression: '"Nouveau riche parvenu"'
    explanation: This phrase emphasizes the newfound wealth and social status of someone

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
