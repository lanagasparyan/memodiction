  word: Incongruous

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Not in harmony or keeping with the surroundings or other aspects of something.
    synonyms:
    - Mismatched
    - Incompatible
    - Unsuitable
    antonyms:
    - Congruous
    - Consistent
    - Suitable
    examples:
    - The modern architecture of the house was incongruous with the historical neighborhood.
    - His casual outfit was incongruous for a formal event.
    mnemonics:
    - Picture mismatched puzzle pieces that don't fit together.
  etymology:
    origin: Late 16th century from Latin 'incongruus', from in- 'not' + congruus 'congruous'.
    historical_usage: The term has retained its meaning since its origin, without
      significant changes in usage.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Fish out of water"'
    explanation: Feeling out of place or uncomfortable in a particular environment
      or situation.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'incongruous' with 'inconsistent'.
    explanation: While both words imply a lack of harmony, 'incongruous' specifically
      refers to something that doesn't fit with its surroundings or aspects, whereas
      'inconsistent' refers to something that lacks uniformity or reliability.
