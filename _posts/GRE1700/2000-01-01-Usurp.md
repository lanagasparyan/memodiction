  - part_of_speech: Verb

    - Seize
    - Take over

    - Relinquish

  etymology:
    origin: Middle English via Old French from Latin 'usurpare', from 'usus' (use)
      + 'rapere' (seize).
    historical_usage: The term 'usurp' originated in the 14th century and has maintained
      a consistent meaning throughout history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Usurp someone''s throne"'
    explanation: To wrongfully take someone's position of power, particularly a monarch
      or ruler.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'usurp' with 'seize' or 'take'.

      to an illegal or forceful acquisition of a position, unlike the more generic
      terms 'seize' or 'take'.
