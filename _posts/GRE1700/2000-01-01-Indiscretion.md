  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Thoughtlessness

    - Prudence
    - Discretion

    - Think of someone who lacks good judgment and often behaves indiscreetly, like
      a gossip columnist.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "indiscretion" originated from the Old French word "indiscretioun"
      and the Latin word "indiscretio", which means "lack of discernment" or "want
      of good judgment."
    historical_usage: The term has been used since the 14th century to refer to actions
      or speech that demonstrate a lack of discretion or good judgment.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Kiss and tell"'
    explanation: Used to describe a situation where someone has indiscreetly revealed

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "indiscretion" with "discretion".
    explanation: '"Indiscretion" refers to behavior or speech lacking good judgment,

      words have opposite meanings.'
