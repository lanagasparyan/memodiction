---
data:
  word: Importune
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To ask someone pressingly and persistently for or to do something; to urge or
      beg.
    synonyms:
    - Beseech
    - Implore
    - Beg
    - Entreat
    antonyms:
    - Discourage
    - Dissuade
    - Deter
    examples:
    - The homeless man importuned passersby for spare change.
    - She importuned her boss for a raise multiple times.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of someone importing their requests persistently.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English 'importunen', from Anglo-Norman French 'importuner', from
      Latin 'importunus' (meaning troublesome, inconvenient)
    historical_usage: The word has been in use since Middle English and its usage
      has remained relatively unchanged.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Importune the heavens"'
    explanation: To plead or beg fervently to the divine or higher powers.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'importune' with 'impugn' or 'impute'.
    explanation: While the words may sound similar, their meanings are quite different.
      'Importune' means to persistently ask or urge, while 'impugn' means to challenge
      or criticize, and 'impute' means to attribute or ascribe.
