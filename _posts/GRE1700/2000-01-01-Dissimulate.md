data:
  word: Dissimulate
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To conceal or disguise one's true thoughts, feelings, or character.
    synonyms:
    - Hide
    - Mask
    - Feign
    antonyms:
    - Reveal
    - Expose
    examples:
    - She dissimulated her disappointment, pretending to be happy for her friend's
      success.
    - He often dissimulates his true intentions, making it difficult to trust him.
    mnemonics:
    - Remember "dis-" prefix usually denotes negation or reversal, and "simulate"
      refers to pretending or imitating. Dissimulate can be thought of as negating
      one's true self.
  etymology:
    origin: Dissimulate derives from the Latin word "dissimulatus," which means "to
      disguise" or "to hide."
    historical_usage: The word has been used in the English language since the late
      16th century, primarily to describe the act of concealing one's thoughts or
      feelings.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Wear a mask"'
    explanation: This phrase metaphorically refers to dissimulating or hiding one's
      true self or intentions.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing dissimulate with simulate.
    explanation: Dissimulate means to hide or disguise, while simulate means to imitate
      or mimic. These two words have opposite meanings, so it is important not to
      confuse them.
