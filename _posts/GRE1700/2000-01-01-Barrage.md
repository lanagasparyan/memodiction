data:
  word: Barrage
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - An artificial barrier across a river or estuary to prevent flooding, aid irrigation
      or navigation, or to generate electricity by tidal power.
    synonyms:
    - Dam
    - Barrier
    - Gate
    - Obstruction
    - Blockade
    antonyms:
    - Opening
    - Passage
    - Clear path
    examples:
    - The construction of a barrage helped control the water flow and prevent flooding
      in the area.
    - The barrage provided irrigation to the surrounding agricultural fields.
    mnemonics:
    - Barrage rhymes with carriage, and just like a carriage is used to transport
      people, a barrage helps to transport water.
  etymology:
    origin: French barrer, meaning 'to bar or obstruct.'
    historical_usage: The word 'barrage' originated in the 19th century and has been
      used consistently to refer to man-made barriers across rivers and estuaries.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Barrage of questions"'
    explanation: Refers to a large number of questions being asked rapidly or in a
      relentless manner.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Associating 'barrage' only with flooding prevention.
    explanation: While preventing flooding is one of the purposes of a barrage, it
      can also aid irrigation, navigation, or generate electricity.
