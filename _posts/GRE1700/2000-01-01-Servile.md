categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Servile
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Having or showing an excessive willingness to serve or please others.
    synonyms:
    - Submissive
    - Obsequious
    - Subservient
    antonyms:
    - Assertive
    - Independent
    - Defiant
    examples:
    - He had a servile attitude towards his boss, always doing whatever was asked
      of him without question.
    - The servants in the mansion were expected to be servile and obedient at all
      times.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of a person bowing down and serving others loyally and obediently.
    - '"Servile" sounds like "serve" and describes someone who is excessively willing
      to serve others.'
  etymology:
    origin: From Middle English "servile", from Old French "servile" or Latin "servilis",
      from "servus" meaning 'servant' or 'slave'.
    historical_usage: The term has been used throughout history to describe individuals
      who are subservient or obedient, often referring to slaves or the lower class.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Servile society"'
    explanation: Refers to a society or culture in which people are expected to be
      extremely submissive and obedient, often lacking freedom or individuality.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "servile" with "service-oriented" or "helpful".
    explanation: While being helpful and service-oriented are positive traits, being
      servile implies an excessive and almost degrading level of subservience without
      considering one's own needs or dignity.
