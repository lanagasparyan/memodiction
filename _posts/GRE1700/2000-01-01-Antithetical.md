  word: Antithetical

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Contrary
    - Opposite
    - Contradictory

    - Similar

  etymology:
    origin: The term "antithetical" is derived from the Greek word "antithetikos,"
      which means "opposite" or "contradictory." It entered the English language in
      the late 16th century.
    historical_usage: '"Antithetical" has been used consistently throughout history
      to describe opposing or contrasting ideas, situations, or beliefs.'
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Poles apart"'
    explanation: This expression conveys the idea of two things being extremely different
      or opposite, similar to the meaning of "antithetical."
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "antithetical" with "synonymous" or "similar."
    explanation: Antithetical means directly opposed or contrasting, while synonymous
      or similar means having similar or related meanings. These terms have distinct
      definitions and should not be used interchangeably.
