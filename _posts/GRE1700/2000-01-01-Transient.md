data:
  word: Transient
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Lasting only for a short time; impermanent.
    - Staying or working in a place for only a short period.
    synonyms:
    - Temporary
    - Fleeting
    - Short-lived
    antonyms:
    - Permanent
    - Enduring
    - Lasting
    examples:
    - The joy of winning the lottery was transient; it quickly faded.
    - The hotel provides accommodation for transient guests.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "transient" as something that is "in transit" or passing through temporarily.
    - The word "transient" rhymes with "distant," which implies something that is
      not lasting or enduring.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "transient" originated from the Latin word "transire," which
      means "to go across" or "to pass." It entered the English language in the mid-17th
      century.
    historical_usage: The term "transient" has been used throughout history to describe
      things or people that are temporary, passing, or not lasting.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Transient nature"'
    explanation: Refers to the inherent impermanence or temporary quality of something.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "transient" with "transcendent."
    explanation: These two words have different meanings. "Transient" refers to something
      temporary or impermanent, while "transcendent" means surpassing or going beyond
      ordinary limits or experiences.
