  word: Acrimonious

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Friendly

    - Think of "acrimony" as a bitter taste, and "acrimonious" refers to a bitter

  etymology:
    origin: Acrimonious comes from the Latin word "acrimoniosus" which means "sharp,
      bitter, stinging".
    historical_usage: The term has been in use since the mid-17th century and has
      retained its meaning of bitterness and hostility.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Acrimonious divorce"'

  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "acrimonious" with "harmonious".
