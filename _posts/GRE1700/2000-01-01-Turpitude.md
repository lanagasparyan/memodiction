  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Wickedness

    - Immorality

    - Virtue

  etymology:
    origin: Turpitude comes from the Latin word "turpitudinem" which means "vileness,
      baseness, moral corruption."
    historical_usage: The word has been in use since the 15th century with its meaning
      relating to moral corruption and depravity.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Sink into turpitude"'
    explanation: Describes a situation where someone becomes morally depraved or corrupt
      over time.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing turpitude with "turbidity."
    explanation: Turbidity refers to the degree to which water loses clarity due to
      the presence of suspended particles. Turpitude, on the other hand, relates to
      immoral or depraved behavior.
