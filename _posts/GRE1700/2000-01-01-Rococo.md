    origin: The term "rococo" originated in the late 18th century from the French

    historical_usage: Rococo emerged in the early 18th century as a reaction against

      Europe, particularly France, during the reign of Louis XV.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Rococo style"'
    explanation: Refers to the artistic and architectural style characterized by intricate

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Rococo is synonymous with Baroque.

      a greater emphasis on asymmetry, delicate ornamentation, and a lighter color
