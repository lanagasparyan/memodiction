---
categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Fidget
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To make small movements, especially of the hands and feet, through nervousness
      or impatience.
    synonyms:
    - Squirm
    - Wriggle
    - Twitch
    antonyms:
    - Stay still
    - Remain calm
    examples:
    - She couldn't help but fidget during the long meeting.
    - The child fidgeted in his seat while waiting for the school bus.
    mnemonics:
    - '''Fidget'' sounds like "fit" and when you''re nervous or impatient, you can''t
      "fit" still.'
  etymology:
    origin: The word "fidget" originated in the mid-18th century and can be traced
      back to the Old English word "fitt," which means a sudden spasm or convulsion.
    historical_usage: Over time, the word evolved to describe the restless, small
      movements associated with nervousness or impatience.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Fidget spinner"'
    explanation: A popular toy consisting of a ball bearing in the center of a multi-lobed
      flat structure designed to be spun on the fingers, providing a distraction for
      those prone to fidgeting.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Fidgeting is always a sign of nervousness.
    explanation: While fidgeting can be associated with nervousness or impatience,
      it can also be a result of restlessness or a natural need for movement. Fidgeting
      does not always indicate anxiety.
