categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Limpid
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - (of a liquid) completely clear and transparent.
    synonyms:
    - Clear
    - Transparent
    - Translucent
    antonyms:
    - Murky
    - Opaque
    - Cloudy
    examples:
    - The limpid waters of the lake reflected the blue sky.
    - Her eyes were limpid pools of blue.
    mnemonics:
    - Imagine a glass of water that is so clear and transparent, it is almost like
      liquid diamonds.
    - Limpid sounds like liquid, just as a clear liquid is limpid.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English "limpid, luminous," from Latin "limpidus," from limpere
      "to be smeared or covered."
    historical_usage: The word has origins in Latin and has been used in English since
      Middle English.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Limpid stream"'
    explanation: Referring to a clear and gently flowing stream, generally used to
      evoke a sense of tranquility or peace.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing limpid with liquid or rapid.
    explanation: Limpid specifically refers to the clarity and transparency of a liquid,
      not to its physical state or speed of flow.
