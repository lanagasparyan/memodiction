    historical_usage: The term has been used since Middle English and was first used
      in religious contexts, referring to the spiritual instruction and moral improvement
      of individuals.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Edify one another"'
    explanation: To provide mutual moral or intellectual guidance and support among
      individuals or a group.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "edify" with "entertain" or "amuse."

      intellectually, rather than simply providing enjoyment or amusement.
