  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Skill
    - Expertise
    - Adroitness
    antonyms:
    - Clumsiness
    - Ineptness
    - Incompetence
    examples:
    - The surgeon displayed remarkable dexterity during the delicate procedure.

    mnemonics:
    - Imagine a juggler showcasing their dexterity by flawlessly tossing and catching
      objects.
  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English from Latin 'dexteritas', from 'dexter' meaning 'right'
      (opposite of 'sinister' meaning 'left').
    historical_usage: The word has been used since the late Middle Ages to refer to
      manual skill or agility.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Digital dexterity"'
    explanation: Refers to the skill and agility in using one's fingers or hands,
      often in the context of technology or fine motor tasks.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing dexterity with speed.
    explanation: Dexterity is not solely about performing tasks quickly, but rather
      about skill and proficiency in performing tasks accurately and effectively.
