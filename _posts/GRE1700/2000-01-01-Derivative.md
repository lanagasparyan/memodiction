---
categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Derivative
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - Something that is derived or obtained from another source.
    - A financial product that derives its value from an underlying asset, index,
      or interest rate.
    synonyms:
    - Spinoff
    - Offshoot
    - Byproduct
    antonyms:
    - Original
    - Innovative
    examples:
    - The movie is a derivative of the popular book series.
    - He invested in derivatives to diversify his portfolio.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Derivative" can be thought of as a "derived" product or idea.'
    - Think of a mathematical derivative, which is a function representing the slope
      of a curve at any given point.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English 'derivative', from Latin 'derivatus', past participle of
      'derivare' meaning 'to derive'.
    historical_usage: The word has been used in various contexts since the Middle
      Ages, evolving to include its modern financial meaning.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Derivative work"'
    explanation: A work based on or derived from one or more existing works, such
      as a sequel, adaptation, or translation.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "derivative" with "derivation".
    explanation: '"Derivative" refers to something derived or obtained from another
      source, while "derivation" refers to the process of deriving or obtaining something.'
