  word: Apropos
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective/Adverb
    definitions:
    - With reference to; concerning.
    - at an opportune time; timely.
    synonyms:
    - Regarding
    - Concerning
    - Relevant
    - Pertinent
    antonyms:
    - Irrelevant
    - Inappropriate
    - Out of context
    examples:
    - Her comments were apropos to the current situation.
    - He made an apropos suggestion during the meeting.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "a pro" who is always relevant and timely in their actions or statements.
    - The word "apropos" sounds similar to "appropriate," which can help remember
      its meaning.
  etymology:
    origin: Apropos comes from the French phrase "à propos" meaning "to the purpose."
    historical_usage: The term "à propos" has been used in French since the 17th century,
      and it entered the English language in the early 18th century. It is commonly
      used in written and spoken English to indicate relevance or timeliness.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Apropos of nothing"'
    explanation: This phrase is used to introduce a statement or topic that is unrelated
      to the current conversation or context.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "apropos" with "appropriate".
    explanation: While "apropos" and "appropriate" may share some similarities in
      meaning, they are not exact synonyms. "Apropos" specifically implies relevance
      or timeliness, while "appropriate" refers to suitability or conformity to a
      particular purpose.
