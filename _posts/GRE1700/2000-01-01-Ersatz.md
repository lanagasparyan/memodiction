data:
  word: Ersatz
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Made or used as a substitute, typically an inferior one, for something else.
    synonyms:
    - Substitute
    - Artificial
    - Imitation
    antonyms:
    - Genuine
    - Authentic
    examples:
    - She served ersatz coffee to her guests.
    - The movie featured an ersatz version of a famous Hollywood star.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "Ersatz" as "er-sad" or "less-pleasing substitute."
  etymology:
    origin: German, from Ersatz 'substitute'
    historical_usage: The term "ersatz" gained popularity during World War I when
      Germany faced shortages and had to use substitutes for various products.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Ersatz religion"'
    explanation: Refers to a belief system or ideology that serves as a substitute
      for traditional or authentic religious practices.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Using ersatz as a synonym for "similar" rather than a substitute.
    explanation: Ersatz implies a lower quality or inferiority compared to the original,
      whereas "similar" suggests resemblance without the negative connotation of substitution.
