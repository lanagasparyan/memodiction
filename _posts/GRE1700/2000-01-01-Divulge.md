data:
  word: Divulge
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To make known (private or sensitive information).
    synonyms:
    - Reveal
    - Disclose
    - Share
    - Impart
    antonyms:
    - Conceal
    - Hide
    - Keep secret
    - Withhold
    examples:
    - She decided to divulge the truth about the company's illegal activities.
    - The detective refused to divulge the identity of the witness.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Divulge" rhymes with "oblige," which can help you remember that revealing
      something can sometimes feel like an obligation.'
  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English from Latin 'divulgare', from 'di-' (thoroughly) +
      'vulgare' (make public).
    historical_usage: The word has been used since late Middle English and has retained
      its primary meaning throughout history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Divulge the beans"'
    explanation: Similar to the phrase "spill the beans," it means to reveal a secret
      or previously unknown information.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "divulge" with "devulge" or "delve."
    explanation: '"Divulge" is the correct term when referring to making private or
      sensitive information known. "Devulge" or "delve" have different meanings and
      should not be used interchangeably.'
