  etymology:
    origin: The word "zealot" comes from the Greek word "zēlōtēs," meaning "to be
      zealous." The term originally referred to a member of a Jewish extremist movement

    historical_usage: In its historical context, "zealot" referred specifically to
      a member of this Jewish extremist movement. However, over time, the term has
      evolved to have a broader meaning, referring to any person who is excessively
      passionate or fanatical about a particular cause or belief.

      beliefs and may impose them on others.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing a zealot with a person who is merely passionate or dedicated
