  etymology:
    origin: The word "pithy" comes from the Middle English word "pithi," which means

    historical_usage: The usage of "pithy" to describe language or style that is concise
      and expressive dates back to the 14th century.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Pithy one-liner"'

      a complete thought or idea.

  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "pithy" with "trite" or "cliché."
