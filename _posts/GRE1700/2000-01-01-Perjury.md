data:
  word: Perjury
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - The offense of willfully telling an untruth or making a misrepresentation under
      oath.
    synonyms:
    - False swearing
    - Lying under oath
    - False testimony
    antonyms:
    - Truthfulness
    - Honesty
    examples:
    - The witness was charged with perjury after giving contradictory statements in
      court.
    - The defense attorney presented evidence to prove that the accusation was based
      on perjury.
    mnemonics:
    - Remember perjury as "per" (meaning against) + "jury" (group of people). It refers
      to the act of lying or deceiving the jury.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English, from Latin "perjurium", from "periurare" (to swear falsely),
      from "per-" (through) + "jurare" (to swear).
    historical_usage: The concept of perjury dates back several centuries and has
      been a crime in many legal systems. It was seen as a serious offense against
      justice.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Commit perjury"'
    explanation: To intentionally testify falsely or make false statements while under
      oath.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Assuming perjury can only occur in a courtroom.
    explanation: Perjury can take place in any situation where a person is legally
      required to tell the truth under oath, such as during a deposition or when providing
      sworn statements. It is not limited to a courtroom setting.
