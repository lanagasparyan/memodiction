data:
  word: Denigrate
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To criticize or speak ill of someone or something unfairly; to belittle or disparage.
    synonyms:
    - Disparage
    - Defame
    - Slander
    - Insult
    - Criticize
    antonyms:
    - Praise
    - Compliment
    - Admire
    - Extol
    examples:
    - She constantly denigrates her colleagues, making them feel inadequate.
    - The article denigrated the achievements of the famous actor, tarnishing his
      reputation.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Denigrate" can be remembered as "De(negative)grate" - negative statements
      or criticism.'
    - Think of "degrading" someone's image or reputation.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "denigrate" is derived from the Latin word "denigratus," which
      means to blacken or darken.
    historical_usage: It has been used since the late 16th century to describe the
      act of disparaging or belittling someone or something.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Denigrate someone''s character"'
    explanation: To make negative comments or remarks about someone's moral qualities
      or reputation.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "denigrate" with "differentiate" or "deteriorate."
    explanation: While these words might sound similar, they have different meanings.
      "Denigrate" refers to unfairly criticizing or belittling someone or something,
      whereas "differentiate" means to distinguish or contrast, and "deteriorate"
      refers to the process of becoming worse or declining in quality.
