categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Ineptitude
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - Lack of skill or ability; incompetence.
    synonyms:
    - Incompetence
    - Incapability
    - Inability
    antonyms:
    - Competence
    - Capability
    - Ability
    examples:
    - His ineptitude in math led to low grades.
    - The team's ineptitude in defense cost them the game.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of the word "in-" as "not" and "eptitude" as "aptitude" or ability, so
      ineptitude means the opposite of ability.
  etymology:
    origin: Ineptitude originated from the Latin word "ineptus" meaning "unsuitable,
      improper," which is derived from "in-" (not) and "aptus" (apt, fit).
    historical_usage: The word has been used in English since the 17th century to
      describe a lack of skill or ability.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Ineptitude personified"'
    explanation: This phrase is used to describe someone who consistently demonstrates
      incompetence or lack of skill.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "ineptitude" with "ineptness."
    explanation: Both words have the same meaning and can be used interchangeably;
      however, "ineptitude" is more commonly used.
