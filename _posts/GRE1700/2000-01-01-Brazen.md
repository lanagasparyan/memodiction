categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Brazen
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Bold and without shame; shamelessly impudent.
    - Made of or resembling brass, especially in color.
    synonyms:
    - Audacious
    - Bold
    - Shameless
    - Impudent
    antonyms:
    - Timid
    - Shy
    - Modest
    - Reserved
    examples:
    - Despite his illegal actions, he had a brazen attitude towards the authorities.
    - The brazen statue gleamed in the sunlight.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of someone displaying a brazen attitude as having a "brass" personality.
    - The bold and shiny appearance of brass can help remember the meaning.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English from Old English bræsen, bræsenan, of Germanic origin;
      related to Old High German berasennen.
    historical_usage: The word has been used since Middle English and has maintained
      its meaning of bold and without shame throughout history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Brazen it out"'
    explanation: To boldly face or endure a difficult or embarrassing situation.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'brazen' with 'brass'.
    explanation: While 'brazen' can describe something made of or resembling brass,
      it predominantly refers to a bold and shameless attitude.
