categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Aversion
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - A strong feeling of dislike or avoidance towards something or someone.
    synonyms:
    - Dislike
    - Hatred
    - Loathing
    - Repulsion
    antonyms:
    - Attraction
    - Fondness
    - Liking
    - Preference
    examples:
    - She has a strong aversion to spiders.
    - He developed an aversion to public speaking after a bad experience.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Aversion" sounds like "avoiding," which is related to the idea of dislike
      or avoidance.'
  etymology:
    origin: From Latin "aversionem" (past participle of "aversare") meaning "to turn
      away from" or "to dislike".
    historical_usage: The word has been used since the 16th century, maintaining its
      meaning of strong dislike or avoidance.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Aversion therapy"'
    explanation: A type of psychological treatment that aims to eliminate undesirable
      behavior by associating it with aversive stimuli to create a negative reaction
      and discourage repetition.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "aversion" with "adversion".
    explanation: '"Aversion" refers to a strong feeling of dislike or avoidance, whereas
      "adversion" means a critical or hostile remark or comment.'
