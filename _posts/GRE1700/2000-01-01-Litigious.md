  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    origin: 'Late Middle English (in the sense ''concerning legal proceedings''):
      from Old French litigieux or late Latin litigiosus, from Latin lites, litig-

    historical_usage: The word "litigious" has been used since the late Middle English
      period to describe someone or something related to legal proceedings or disputes.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"A litigious society"'
    explanation: Refers to a society or community where legal actions and disputes
      are common, frequently resulting in lawsuits.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "litigious" with "litigant."

      legal proceeding. They are related terms but have distinct meanings.
