  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Newly rich
    antonyms:
    - Old money

      used pejoratively to describe people who recently gained wealth and were seen
      as lacking the refinement and class associated with old-established families.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Nouveau riche lifestyle"'

  - region: ''

  - misconception: Confusing "nouveau riche" with "wealthy" or "rich."
