categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Broach
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To raise or introduce (a difficult or sensitive subject) for discussion or consideration.
    synonyms:
    - Bring up
    - Introduce
    - Mention
    - Raise
    - Propose
    antonyms:
    - Avoid
    - Ignore
    - Suppress
    examples:
    - She broached the topic of salary during the job interview.
    - He finally broached the idea of getting a pet to his parents.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "broaching the subject" as opening up a conversation about a difficult
      topic.
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English 'brochen', from Old French 'brochier', meaning "to pierce"
      or "to tap."
    historical_usage: The term originally referred to the act of piercing a cask to
      draw out its contents. In the early 16th century, it began to be used metaphorically
      to mean introducing a topic or bringing something up for discussion.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Broach the subject"'
    explanation: This phrase means to initiate or bring up a sensitive or difficult
      topic for discussion.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'broach' with 'brooch'.
    explanation: '''Brooch'' refers to a piece of jewelry worn on clothing, while
      ''broach'' means to initiate a discussion. These are two distinct words with
      different meanings.'
