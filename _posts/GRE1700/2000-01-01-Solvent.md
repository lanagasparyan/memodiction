data:
  word: Solvent
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Having assets in excess of liabilities; able to pay one's debts.
    synonyms:
    - Financially secure
    - Well-off
    - Creditworthy
    antonyms:
    - Insolvent
    - Bankrupt
    examples:
    - Despite the economic downturn, the company remained solvent.
    - She worked hard to become solvent and pay off her debts.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of 'solvency' as the ability to be financially secure and stable.
  etymology:
    origin: From Latin 'solvēre' meaning 'to loosen, dissolve'.
    historical_usage: The term was initially used in accounting and financial contexts
      since the late 17th century.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Stay solvent"'
    explanation: To maintain one's financial stability and avoid being in debt or
      bankruptcy.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'solvent' with 'solvable'.
    explanation: While both words involve finding a solution, 'solvent' refers to
      financial stability, while 'solvable' means capable of being solved or resolved.
