data:
  word: Supine
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Lying on the back or with the face upward.
    - Failing to act or protest as a result of moral weakness or indolence.
    synonyms:
    - Recumbent
    - Prone
    - Inactive
    - Idle
    antonyms:
    - Active
    - Upright
    - Diligent
    - Energetic
    examples:
    - After a tiring day, she lay supine on the grass and looked up at the stars.
    - The government's supine response to the crisis was criticized by the public.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of the word "supine" as "soup-on" where someone is lazily lying down,
      not taking any action.
  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English, from Latin 'supinus', related to 'sub-' (meaning
      'up') and 'ponere' (meaning 'to place').
    historical_usage: The term 'supine' has been used since the late 15th century
      to refer to lying on the back. The figurative usage to describe moral weakness
      or inaction emerged later.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Supine position"'
    explanation: Refers to the posture of lying on one's back, often used in medical
      and healthcare contexts.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'supine' with 'prone'.
    explanation: While both terms refer to lying down, 'supine' specifically describes
      the position of lying on the back, whereas 'prone' refers to lying face down.
