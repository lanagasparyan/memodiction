  idioms:
  - expression: '"Hold an inquest"'
    explanation: Refers to initiating or convening an official inquiry or investigation,
      usually by a coroner or similar authority.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing an inquest with a trial.
