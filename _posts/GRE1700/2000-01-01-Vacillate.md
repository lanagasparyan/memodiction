    - Oscillate
    - Fluctuate
    - Waver
    - Dither

    origin: The word "vacillate" comes from the Latin word "vacillatus" which means
      "to sway to and fro". It is derived from the Latin word "vacillare" which means

    historical_usage: The concept of vacillation has been present in human behavior
      for centuries, with the word itself dating back to late Middle English.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Vacillate between two choices"'

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "vacillate" with "vacuum" or "vaccinate".
