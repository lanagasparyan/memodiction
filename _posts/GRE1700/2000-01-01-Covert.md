    - Secret
    - Undercover

    - Overt

    examples:

    origin: Late Middle English from Old French 'covert' or Latin 'cooperire', from

    historical_usage: The term originated in the late 15th century, related to covering
      or concealing.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Covert operations"'
    explanation: Activities carried out by intelligence services or military units
      that are concealed or hidden from public knowledge.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'covert' with 'overt'.
