  word: Modish

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Trendy

    - Stylish
    - In vogue

    - Outdated

    - Unfashionable
    examples:

  etymology:
    origin: Middle English "modish", from mode and -ish suffix.
    historical_usage: The term "modish" has been used since the 16th century to describe
      fashionable and trendy trends.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"In the modish crowd"'
    explanation: Referring to being part of the fashionable and trendy group or social
      circle.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "modish" with "modern".
    explanation: While "modish" shares a similar concept of being current and fashionable
      as "modern," it specifically refers to conforming to current trends rather than
      a specific time period or style.
