data:
  word: Reticent
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Not revealing one's thoughts or feelings readily; reserved or restrained.
    synonyms:
    - Reserved
    - Withdrawn
    - Introverted
    antonyms:
    - Outgoing
    - Talkative
    - Expressive
    examples:
    - She was reticent about her personal life and rarely shared any details.
    - The reticent student rarely participated in class discussions.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of someone holding back their words, hence being reticent.
    - Reticent sounds like "silent," which can remind you of someone being quiet and
      not speaking up readily.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "reticent" comes from the Latin word "reticentem", which is the
      present participle of "reticere" meaning to keep silent or to be silent. "Reticent"
      entered English in the late 19th century.
    historical_usage: The term has been used consistently throughout its history to
      describe someone who is reserved or hesitant to share their thoughts.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Speak if you''re spoken to"'
    explanation: A phrase that highlights the expectation of only speaking when directly
      addressed, reflecting the reticence to initiate conversation.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "reticent" with "reluctant".
    explanation: While both words relate to hesitation or reservation, "reticent"
      specifically refers to not revealing one's thoughts or feelings, while "reluctant"
      pertains to being unwilling or hesitant to do something.
