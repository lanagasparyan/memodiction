  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Hedonist
    antonyms:
    - Moralist
    - Puritan
    - Ascetic

    - Think of the word 'libertine' as derived from 'liberty', emphasizing the freedom

  etymology:
    origin: The term 'libertine' can be traced back to the late 16th century and is

      It stems from the Latin word 'libertinus', which is related to 'libertas' meaning
      'freedom'.
    historical_usage: Libertinism became especially prominent during the Restoration
      Era in England, where it was associated with the pleasure-seeking lifestyle
      of the wealthy upper class. However, the concept of libertinism can be found
      in various historical periods.

  - expression: '"Live like a libertine"'
