data:
  word: Eulogy
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - A speech or piece of writing that praises someone or something highly, especially
      a tribute to someone who has just died.
    synonyms:
    - Tribute
    - Praise
    - Commendation
    - Accolade
    antonyms:
    - Criticism
    - Condemnation
    examples:
    - The eulogy delivered at her funeral was heartfelt and moving.
    - He wrote a beautiful eulogy for his grandfather's memorial service.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Eulogy" sounds like "you-logy" - a speech about someone''s life story.'
  etymology:
    origin: The word "eulogy" comes from the Greek word "eulogia," which means praise
      or blessing.
    historical_usage: The tradition of delivering eulogies dates back centuries and
      has been an important part of funeral rituals in many cultures around the world.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Deliver a eulogy"'
    explanation: To give a speech in honor of someone who has passed away.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing a eulogy with a speech at a funeral or memorial service.
    explanation: While a eulogy is often delivered at a funeral or memorial service,
      it specifically refers to a speech that praises and celebrates the life of the
      deceased. Not all speeches at such events are eulogies; some may be remembrances
      or tributes that focus on different aspects or memories.
