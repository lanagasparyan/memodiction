data:
  word: Ancillary
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Providing necessary support to the primary activities or operation of an organization,
      system, etc.
    synonyms:
    - Auxiliary
    - Supplementary
    - Supporting
    antonyms:
    - Primary
    - Main
    - Principal
    examples:
    - The administrative staff plays an ancillary role in the smooth functioning of
      the company.
    - The new software is an ancillary component to the existing infrastructure.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of the word "ancillary" as something additional and supportive.
  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English (in the sense 'subordinate'), from Latin 'ancillaris',
      from 'ancilla' meaning "female servant" or "handmaid".
    historical_usage: The word has been used to describe secondary or supportive roles
      since its origin.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Ancillary to"'
    explanation: Referring to something that is supplementary or supportive to the
      main focus or purpose.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "ancillary" with "ancient" due to similar sounds.
    explanation: While they sound similar, "ancillary" and "ancient" have completely
      different meanings. "Ancillary" refers to support or extra, while "ancient"
      relates to something that is old or from a long time ago.
