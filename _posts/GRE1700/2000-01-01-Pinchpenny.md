  etymology:
    origin: The word "pinchpenny" originated in the early 17th century in English.

      money. The term was commonly used to describe people who were excessively frugal
      or stingy.
    historical_usage: The term has been used throughout history to describe individuals
      who are known for their reluctance to spend money and their extreme thriftiness.

  misconceptions:

      spend money when necessary or being unreasonably stingy.
