data:
  word: Deprecate
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To express disapproval of something; to belittle or criticize.
    synonyms:
    - Condemn
    - Denounce
    - Censure
    antonyms:
    - Praise
    - Commend
    - Approve
    examples:
    - The teacher deprecated the student's lazy attitude towards studying.
    - She deprecated his decision to quit his job without another lined up.
    mnemonics:
    - Remember that the prefix "de-" in "deprecate" means "down" or "remove," implying
      a negative judgment.
  etymology:
    origin: Late 15th century; from Latin 'deprecatus', past participle of 'deprecari'
      meaning "to pray against," from 'de-' meaning "away" + 'precari' meaning "to
      pray."
    historical_usage: Originally used in a religious context, expressing a plea for
      protection or deliverance from evil or harm. Over time, it developed a broader
      usage to convey expressing disapproval or criticism.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Deprecate oneself"'
    explanation: Refers to a form of self-disapproval or self-criticism.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "deprecate" with "depreciate."
    explanation: '"Deprecate" refers to expressing disapproval, while "depreciate"
      refers to the decline in value or importance of something. They are distinct
      words with different meanings, although their spellings are similar.'
