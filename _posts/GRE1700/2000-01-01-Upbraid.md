  word: Upbraid

  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To find fault with (someone); scold or criticize.
    - To reproach or reprimand severely.
    synonyms:
    - Berate
    - Admonish
    - Reprove
    - Chastise
    antonyms:
    - Praise
    - Commend
    - Applaud
    examples:
    - The teacher upbraided the student for not completing the assignment on time.

    mnemonics:
    - '''Upbraid'' sounds like "up + braid." When you braid someone''s hair, you may

  etymology:
    origin: Middle English 'upbreyden', from Old English 'ūpbregdan' (compound of
      'ūp' meaning up and 'bregdan' meaning to move quickly) + the suffix '-en'. The
      meaning has shifted over time from 'to move quickly upwards' to 'to reproach
      or scold'.
    historical_usage: The word has been used since Middle English and has retained
      its primary meaning.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Upbraid oneself"'
    explanation: To criticize or reproach oneself for something.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'upbraid' with 'uphold'.
    explanation: '''Upbraid'' means to criticize or scold, while ''uphold'' means

      used interchangeably.'
