    - Impertinence

    - Disrespect

    - '"Insolence" sounds like "in-silence," and when someone is insolent, they are

  etymology:
    origin: The word "insolence" originated from the Latin word "insolentia," which
      means "immoderation, an undue presumption of liberty, arrogance."
    historical_usage: The term has been used to describe rude and disrespectful behavior
      throughout history, often associated with a lack of humility or a disregard
      for authority.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"To show insolence"'
    explanation: This phrase is used to describe the act of displaying disrespectful
      behavior or attitude.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing insolence with aggression or assertiveness.

      not be disrespectful. Insolence implies a rude and disrespectful tone or attitude.
