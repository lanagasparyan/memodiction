    origin: The word "touchstone" comes from Middle English "touche-stone" where "touche"

    historical_usage: The term "touchstone" originally referred to a dark, siliceous
      stone used to test the purity of gold or silver by rubbing against it. Over
      time, the word evolved to represent a metaphorical testing or judging tool.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Pass the touchstone"'

  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "touchstone" with "touch screen."
