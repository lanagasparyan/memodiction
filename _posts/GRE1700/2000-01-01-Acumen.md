data:
  word: Acumen
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - The ability to make good judgments and quick decisions, typically in a particular
      domain or field.
    synonyms:
    - Astuteness
    - Sharpness
    - Shrewdness
    - Keenness
    antonyms:
    - Foolishness
    - Stupidity
    - Ignorance
    examples:
    - His business acumen helped him build a successful company.
    - The lawyer's legal acumen allowed her to win the case.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "A-cu-men" as "Able to judge and make decisions quickly."
  etymology:
    origin: Acumen is derived from the Latin word "acūmen" which means sharpness or
      point. The word was first recorded in English in the 16th century.
    historical_usage: Acumen has been used in English since the 1500s to refer to
      mental sharpness and the ability to make good judgments.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Business acumen"'
    explanation: Refers to the ability to make sound business decisions and judgments.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing acumen with intelligence or knowledge.
    explanation: Acumen specifically refers to the ability to make good judgments
      and quick decisions, not just having knowledge or intelligence.
