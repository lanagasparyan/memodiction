    origin: The word "pedagogy" comes from the Greek word "paidagōgia" which means
      "the function of a paidagōgos," referring to a male slave who accompanied boys
      to school.
    historical_usage: The term "pedagogy" has been used in educational contexts since
      ancient Greece, evolving over time to encompass various theories and approaches
      to teaching.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Pedagogy of the oppressed"'
    explanation: Coined by Brazilian educator Paulo Freire, this phrase refers to
      a teaching approach aimed at empowering marginalized individuals and promoting

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
