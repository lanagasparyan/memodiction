  - part_of_speech: Noun

    synonyms:
    - Walker

    antonyms:
    - Driver
    - Motorist
    examples:

    - A pedestrian is someone on foot, like a "pedestal" is something a statue stands
      on.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "pedestrian" comes from the Latin word "pedester," which means
      "on foot." It entered the English language in the late 18th century.
    historical_usage: The term has consistently referred to someone walking as opposed
      to traveling in a vehicle throughout its history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Pedestrian crossing"'
    explanation: Refers to a designated area, typically marked by painted lines, where
      pedestrians can safely cross a road or street.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Using "pedestrian" to refer to something boring or uninteresting.
    explanation: While the word is sometimes used metaphorically to describe something

      a vehicle. This usage as a metaphorical term should not be confused with its
      literal meaning.
