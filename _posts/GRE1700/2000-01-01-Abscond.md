  word: Abscond
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To leave hurriedly and secretly, typically to avoid detection of or arrest for
      an unlawful action.
    synonyms:
    - Escape
    - Flee
    - Run away
    antonyms:
    - Stay
    - Remain
    - Abide
    examples:
    - The thief absconded with the stolen jewels before the police arrived.
    - The employee absconded with classified documents to sell them to a rival company.
    mnemonics:
    - '''Abscond'' can be remembered by breaking it down as ''ab-'' (away) and ''-scond''
      (sound), implying leaving secretly and avoiding detection like a sound fading
      away.'
  etymology:
    origin: The word 'abscond' originated in the late 16th century from Latin 'abscondere',
      meaning 'to hide.'
    historical_usage: '''Abscond'' has been used to describe fleeing or escaping since
      its origin, especially in legal contexts.'
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Abscond with"'
    explanation: This phrase means to take something unlawfully or secretly while
      fleeing or escaping. It implies stealing or misappropriating something while
      making a clandestine exit.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'abscond' with 'absolve.'
    explanation: '''Abscond'' and ''absolve'' have different meanings and should not
      be used interchangeably. ''Abscond'' refers to leaving or escaping, while ''absolve''
      means to free from guilt or responsibility.'
