    - Foolish

    - Idiotic

    - Wise

    origin: The word "fatuous" comes from the Latin word "fatuus," meaning foolish

    historical_usage: The term has been in use since the late 17th century and has
      retained its meaning throughout its history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Fatuous grin"'
    explanation: This phrase refers to a foolish or silly smile, often implying unawareness

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "fatuous" with "facetious."
