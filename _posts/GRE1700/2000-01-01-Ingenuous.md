data:
  word: Ingenuous
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Innocent, naive, and unsuspecting.
    - Candid, frank, and open in character or nature.
    synonyms:
    - Innocent
    - Naive
    - Guileless
    - Artless
    antonyms:
    - Cunning
    - Crafty
    - Deceitful
    - Sly
    examples:
    - The child's ingenuous remarks brought smiles to everyone's faces.
    - She gave an ingenuous account of the incident, without any attempts at manipulation.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "genius" without the "gen" - indicating someone who is not cunning
      or deceitful.
    - In-genius - suggesting a lack of cunning or manipulation.
  etymology:
    origin: From Latin "ingenuus" meaning native, free-born, or sincere.
    historical_usage: The word has its roots in ancient Rome, where "ingenuus" was
      used to describe someone who was born free (not a slave) and sincere in character.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Ingenuous smile"'
    explanation: Refers to a natural and genuine smile that reflects innocence and
      sincerity.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "ingenuous" with "ingenious."
    explanation: While the two words sound similar, they have distinct meanings. "Ingenuous"
      refers to innocence and sincerity, whereas "ingenious" refers to cleverness
      and intelligence.
