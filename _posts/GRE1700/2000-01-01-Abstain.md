  - part_of_speech: Verb

    - Refrain
    - Desist
    - Avoid
    - Forgo

    - Indulge
    - Partake

  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English from Old French abstiner, from Latin abstinent- 'withholding
      oneself', from abstinere, from ab- 'from' + tenere 'to hold'.
    historical_usage: The word has been used since the late Middle English period
      and originated from French and Latin roots.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Abstain from voting"'
    explanation: Refrain from casting a vote or participating in a vote.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "abstain" with "refrain" or "avoid."
    explanation: While "refrain" and "avoid" have similar meanings, "abstain" specifically
      refers to restraining oneself from doing or enjoying something, often used in
      the context of deliberately not indulging in a particular activity or pleasure.
