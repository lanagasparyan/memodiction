  word: Herald

  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - An official messenger who announces important news or events.
    synonyms:
    - Messenger
    - Announcer
    - Crier
    antonyms:
    - None
    examples:
    - The herald proclaimed the arrival of the king.

    mnemonics:
    - '"Herald" sounds like "heralding," which means announcing or proclaiming.'
  etymology:
    origin: Late Old English 'her(e)ald', from Old French 'heraud', from Frankish
      'hariwald', from Germanic 'harja' (army) and 'waldan' (to rule)
    historical_usage: Heralds were originally messengers sent by monarchs to convey
      important messages. Eventually, they became official announcers of events and
      functions.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Herald of doom"'
    explanation: Refers to a person or thing viewed as a sign that something bad or
      disastrous is about to happen.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "herald" with "heraldry."
    explanation: '"Herald" refers to a messenger or announcer, while "heraldry" refers
      to the system by which coats of arms and other armorial bearings are devised,
      described, and regulated.'
