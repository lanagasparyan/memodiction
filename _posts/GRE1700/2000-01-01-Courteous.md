  etymology:
    origin: 'Middle English: from Old French ''curteis'', based on Latin ''curtus''

    historical_usage: The word 'courteous' has been used since Middle English and

  idioms:

    explanation: Refers to basic polite behavior that is expected from everyone in

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'courteous' with 'cordial' or 'friendly'.
