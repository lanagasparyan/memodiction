    origin: The word "improvident" originated from the Latin word "improvidus," which
      combines the prefix "in-" (meaning "not") and the word "providēre" (meaning
      "to foresee").
    historical_usage: The term has been in use since the late 16th century, retaining
      its meaning of lacking foresight and financial prudence.

  misconceptions:
