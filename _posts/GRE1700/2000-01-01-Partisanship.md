    origin: The word "partisanship" originated in the early 19th century from "partisan,"

      biased support for a specific political party or ideology.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Blind partisanship"'

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "partisanship" with "participation."
