---
categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Repertoire
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - A stock of plays, dances, or items that a company or a performer knows or is
      prepared to perform.
    synonyms:
    - Collection
    - Stock
    - Selection
    antonyms:
    - None
    examples:
    - The theater group has an extensive repertoire of classic and contemporary plays.
    - The orchestra performed a diverse repertoire of symphonies and concertos.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of repertoire as a collection or stock of items that a performer knows.
  etymology:
    origin: French, from late Latin 'repertorium' meaning "inventory, list," ultimately
      from Latin 'reperire' meaning "to find"
    historical_usage: The term repertoire has been used in English since the late
      18th century, borrowed from French where it was used primarily in the context
      of theater and performing arts.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Add to one''s repertoire"'
    explanation: To acquire additional knowledge, skills, or performance pieces.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing the term repertoire with repertory.
    explanation: While both terms are related to the field of performing arts, they
      have slightly different meanings. Repertory refers to a theater company or a
      series of performances, while repertoire specifically refers to the stock or
      collection of plays, dances, or items that can be performed by an individual
      or a company.
