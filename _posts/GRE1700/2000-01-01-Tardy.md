  word: Tardy
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Delaying or delayed beyond the right or expected time; late.
    synonyms:
    - Late
    - Overdue
    - Behind schedule
    antonyms:
    - Punctual
    - Early
    - On time
    examples:
    - The student was reprimanded for being tardy to class.
    - He missed the bus because of his tardy departure.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of the word "tar" which is sticky and slows you down. Being tardy means
      you're "stuck" in time and running late.
    - Imagine a turtle (tardle) walking slowly and always being late.
  etymology:
    origin: The adjective "tardy" originated in the late 14th century from the Old
      French word "tardif", which is derived from the Latin word "tardus" meaning
      "slow, sluggish".
    historical_usage: '"Tardy" has remained relatively consistent in its meaning and
      usage throughout history.'
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Tardy slip"'
    explanation: A written note or official document issued to a student who arrives
      late to school or a class.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Believing "tardy" is synonymous with "lazy".
    explanation: '"Tardy" refers to being late or delayed, while "lazy" refers to
      a lack of effort or unwillingness to work. They are distinct concepts with different
      meanings.'
