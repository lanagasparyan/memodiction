  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Friendly

    - Affable
    - Gracious

  etymology:
    origin: From the Latin word "cordialis", meaning "of the heart".
    historical_usage: The term 'cordial' was originally used in contexts related to
      the heart and emotions. Over time, it evolved to describe warm and friendly
      interactions between people.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Cordial relations"'
    explanation: Refers to friendly and cooperative interactions between individuals,
      organizations, or nations.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'cordial' with 'formal'.
    explanation: While cordiality can be displayed in formal situations, it does not

      and friendly demeanor, which can be present in both formal and informal contexts.
