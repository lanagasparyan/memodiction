data:
  word: Sinuous
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Having many curves and turns; winding.
    synonyms:
    - Curvy
    - Twisting
    - Meandering
    antonyms:
    - Straight
    - Direct
    examples:
    - The sinuous road led us through breathtaking scenic landscapes.
    - She gracefully moved her sinuous body to the rhythm of the music.
    mnemonics:
    - Imagine a sinuous snake slithering through a twisting path.
  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English from Latin 'sinuosus', from 'sinus' (a bend, curve)
    historical_usage: The word has been used to describe curving and winding objects
      or pathways since its introduction in the English language.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Sinuous river"'
    explanation: Refers to a river with many twists and turns along its course.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'sinuous' with 'sensuous'.
    explanation: Despite their similar spellings, 'sinuous' and 'sensuous' have different
      meanings. 'Sinuous' refers to having many curves and turns, while 'sensuous'
      refers to something that is related to or affects the senses, especially pleasurable
      sensations.
