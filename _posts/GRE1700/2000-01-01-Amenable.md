    - Compliant

    - Tractable

    - Resistant

    origin: Late 16th century from Latin 'amœnabilis', from 'amœnus' meaning 'pleasant'.
    historical_usage: The word has retained its meaning of being open and responsive
      to suggestion since its origin.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Amenable to reason"'
    explanation: Willing to consider logical arguments or be persuaded based on rationality.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "amenable" with "agreeable."
    explanation: While they share some similarities, amenable specifically refers
      to being responsive to suggestion, while agreeable refers more generally to
      being pleasant or easy to get along with.
