  word: Unflagging

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    synonyms:
    - Unwavering
    - Relentless
    - Untiring
    - Inexhaustible
    antonyms:
    - Flagging

    - Exhausted

    examples:
    - Despite their challenging circumstances, the team showed unflagging determination

    - Her unflagging energy and dedication to her work impressed everyone around her.

    - Think of the word "flag," and how when you're tired, your energy or enthusiasm

  etymology:
    origin: The word "unflagging" is derived from the verb "flag" with the prefix
      "un-," which denotes negation or reversal.
    historical_usage: The word has been used in English since the late 18th century
      to describe continuous effort or unwavering dedication.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Unflagging support"'
    explanation: This phrase describes unwavering and constant support given to someone
      or something.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "unflagging" with "flagging."
    explanation: '"Unflagging" means tireless and persistent, while "flagging" means
