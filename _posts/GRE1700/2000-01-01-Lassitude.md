categories: [GRE, GRE_SET_2, TOEFL, English]
data:
  word: Lassitude
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - A state of physical or mental weariness; lack of energy.
    synonyms:
    - Fatigue
    - Lethargy
    - Tiredness
    antonyms:
    - Energy
    - Vitality
    - Vigor
    examples:
    - After a long day of work, she could not shake off her lassitude.
    - The illness left him with a constant feeling of lassitude.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Lassitude" sounds like "lastitude," as in being at the end of your energy
      or stamina.'
  etymology:
    origin: The word "lassitude" originated in the early 18th century, derived from
      the Latin word "lassus" meaning weary or tired.
    historical_usage: The term has been used since the 18th century to describe feelings
      of weariness or exhaustion.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"In the grip of lassitude"'
    explanation: Being completely engulfed or controlled by a state of weariness or
      lack of energy.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "lassitude" with "laziness" or lack of motivation.
    explanation: While lassitude can result in feelings of tiredness or lack of energy,
      it is not the same as laziness, which implies a lack of desire or willingness
      to do work or activities. Lassitude is often caused by physical or mental fatigue
      rather than a lack of motivation.
