  word: Turbid
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Cloudy or opaque in appearance, often due to suspension of insoluble matter.
    - Confused or obscure in meaning or effect.
    synonyms:
    - Cloudy
    - Murky
    - Muddy
    antonyms:
    - Clear
    - Transparent
    examples:
    - The river water was turbid after the heavy rain.
    - His turbid writing style made it difficult to understand his ideas.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of a clouded, turbid water.
    - Remember that turbid can also refer to something unclear or confusing.
  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English from Latin 'turbidus', from 'turbare' meaning 'to
      disturb' or 'agitate'.
    historical_usage: The word "turbid" originated in the late Middle English period
      and has been used consistently since then to describe cloudiness or confusion.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Turbid waters"'
    explanation: Refers to a situation or circumstance that is unclear, confusing,
      or filled with turmoil.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing the meaning of turbid with "turgid".
    explanation: While "turbid" refers to cloudiness or confusion, "turgid" means
      inflated, pompous, or excessively ornate in style or language. These are two
      distinct words with different meanings and should not be interchanged.
