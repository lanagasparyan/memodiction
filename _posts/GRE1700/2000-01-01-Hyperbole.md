  - part_of_speech: Noun

    - Overstatement
    - Exaggeration
    - Magnification
    - Embellishment
    antonyms:
    - Understatement
    - Downplaying
    examples:
    - The politician's claim that he could solve all the country's problems overnight

    - She used hyperbole to describe the size of the fish she caught, saying it was

    - Hyperbole can be remembered as "hype" and "bowl". Imagine someone hyping up

    origin: The word "hyperbole" comes from the Greek word "hyperbolḗ" which means
      "excess" or "overcasting".

      to create emphasis, grab attention, and add dramatic effect.

  - expression: '"I''m so hungry I could eat a horse!"'
