  word: Largesse

  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - Generosity in bestowing money or gifts upon others.
    synonyms:
    - Generosity
    - Benevolence
    - Munificence
    - Liberality
    antonyms:
    - Stinginess
    - Selfishness
    - Frugality
    examples:
    - The billionaire showed great largesse by donating millions to charity.

    mnemonics:
    - Remember "large" and associate it with generosity on a grand scale.
    - Think of "lard" or fat, representing abundance and generosity.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "largesse" originated from the Old French term "largesse" meaning
      "gift, present". It can be traced back to the Latin word "largus" which means
      "abundant" or "ample".
    historical_usage: The concept of largesse was prevalent in medieval times when
      feudal lords bestowed gifts or money upon their vassals as a sign of patronage
      or magnanimity.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Largesse of spirit"'
    explanation: Refers to a person's generous and open-hearted nature, suggesting
      that they are not only generous with material things but also with their time,
      understanding, and empathy.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing largesse with mere monetary wealth.
    explanation: Largesse is not solely related to the act of giving money or gifts,
      but also emphasizes the spirit of generosity and benevolence behind the act.
