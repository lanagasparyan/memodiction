data:
  word: Commuter
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Noun
    definitions:
    - a person who travels some distance to work on a regular basis.
    synonyms:
    - Traveler
    - Passenger
    - Commuter
    antonyms:
    - Resident
    examples:
    - Sarah is a commuter who travels by train to her office in the city every day.
    - The commuter train arrived at the station right on time.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "commuter" as someone who commutes regularly for work.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "commuter" originated in the early 19th century from the verb
      "commute," which means to travel regularly between two points, especially from
      home to work or vice versa. It is derived from the Latin word "commutare," which
      means "to change."
    historical_usage: The term "commuter" was initially used in relation to transportation
      and started gaining popularity during the Industrial Revolution when people
      started moving to cities for work and commuting became a common practice.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Commuter belt"'
    explanation: Refers to the suburban areas surrounding a city where commuters reside
      and travel to work in the city.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Assuming that all commuters use public transportation.
    explanation: While many commuters use public transportation, some may use their
      own vehicles or carpool for their daily commute. Commuting refers to the act
      of traveling regularly to work, regardless of the mode of transportation.
