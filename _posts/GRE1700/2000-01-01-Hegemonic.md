  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    - Dominant

    - Subservient

    origin: The word "hegemonic" derives from the Greek word "hegemonikos," which
      means "ruling" or "dominant."
    historical_usage: The term "hegemonic" emerged in the mid-20th century within
      the field of international relations, initially referring to the dominance of
      a single state or power over others.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Hegemonic power"'
    explanation: Refers to a country or entity that holds significant influence and

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "hegemonic" with "hegemony."

      actual dominance or control itself.
