  etymology:
    origin: Late Middle English, from Latin 'praecludere', from prae- (meaning before)
      + claudere (meaning to close, shut).
    historical_usage: The word has been used since the late Middle English period
      and its meaning has remained relatively consistent.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Preclude the possibility"'
    explanation: Refers to preventing the chance or opportunity of something happening.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "preclude" with "exclude".
    explanation: While both words imply preventing or not allowing something, "preclude"
      specifically means to make something impossible or prevent it from happening,
      while "exclude" means to leave out or not include.
