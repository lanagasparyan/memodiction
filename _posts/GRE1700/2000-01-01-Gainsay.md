    historical_usage: The word has been used in English since the Middle Ages, maintaining
      its original meaning of contradicting or speaking against.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Without gainsaying"'
    explanation: Indicates agreement or acceptance without objection.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing 'gainsay' with 'disagree'.
