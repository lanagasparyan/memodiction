    synonyms:
    - Axiom
    - Maxim
    - Aphorism
    - Proverb
    antonyms: []

    - '"The dictum ''practice makes perfect'' is often quoted in relation to achieving

    - '"The dictum ''honesty is the best policy'' is a popular saying."'
    mnemonics:
    - Think of a "ditto" dictum, meaning a statement worth repeating.
  etymology:
    origin: Latin word "dictum" which means a saying or phrase.
    historical_usage: The term has been used since the 16th century to refer to authoritative
      or decisive statements made by someone in a position of power or knowledge.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Lay down the dictum"'
    explanation: To assert or establish a rule or principle in a commanding manner.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing dictum with dicta or dictums with dicta.
