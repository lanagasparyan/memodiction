data:
  word: Voluble
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - (of a person) talking fluently, readily, or incessantly.
    synonyms:
    - Talkative
    - Loquacious
    - Garrulous
    antonyms:
    - Taciturn
    - Reticent
    - Quiet
    examples:
    - She was known for being voluble, always dominating conversations.
    - The salesman's voluble nature helped him sell his products effectively.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Voluble" sounds like "voluminous," which means having great volume or quantity.
      A voluble person often speaks in large quantities or volumes.'
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English, via Old French from Latin 'volutibilis ', from volvere
      'to roll, turn'.
    historical_usage: The word has been used since Middle English and remains common
      in modern usage.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Voluble tongue"'
    explanation: Refers to someone who speaks quickly and continuously, often without
      thinking or pausing.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "voluble" with "volatile."
    explanation: These words have different meanings and should not be used interchangeably.
      "Voluble" refers to being talkative, while "volatile" describes instability
      or unpredictability.
