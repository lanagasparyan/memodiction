  word: Affluent

  - part_of_speech: Adjective

    synonyms:
    - Wealthy
    - Prosperous
    - Rich
    - Well-off
    antonyms:
    - Impoverished
    - Poor
    - Destitute
    examples:
    - The affluent neighborhood showcased grand mansions and luxury cars.
    - The company's affluent clients demanded top-notch services.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "affluent" as "a lot of wealth."
  etymology:
    origin: The word "affluent" originated in the mid-16th century from the Latin
      word "affluere," which means "to flow to" or "to abound in."
    historical_usage: Originally, "affluent" referred to a flowing or abundance of
      something. Over time, it came to be associated with wealth and prosperity.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Affluent society"'
    explanation: Refers to a society characterized by a high standard of living and
      material wealth.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "affluent" with "influential."
    explanation: While both words convey power and status, "affluent" specifically
      refers to wealth and financial prosperity, whereas "influential" refers to having
      the ability to influence or affect others.
