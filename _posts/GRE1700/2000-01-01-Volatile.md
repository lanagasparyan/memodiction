data:
  word: Volatile
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Liable to change rapidly and unpredictably, especially for the worse.
    - Evaporating rapidly at normal temperatures.
    - (Chemistry) Containing substances that evaporate readily at normal temperatures.
    synonyms:
    - Unpredictable
    - Unstable
    - Erratic
    - Unsettled
    - Changeable
    antonyms:
    - Stable
    - Constant
    - Steady
    - Predictable
    examples:
    - The stock market can be volatile, with prices fluctuating wildly.
    - Acetone is a volatile liquid that evaporates quickly.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of "volatility" as something that can quickly change, like a roller coaster
      ride.
    - The word "volatile" sounds like "volcano," which erupts unpredictably.
  etymology:
    origin: Late Latin 'volatilis', from Latin 'volare' meaning "to fly".
    historical_usage: The word originated from Latin and has been used in English
      since the late 17th century with a similar meaning related to rapid evaporation.
      Its usage expanded to include the sense of rapid and unpredictable change in
      the 19th century.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Walking on a volatile ground"'
    explanation: Refers to a situation that is unpredictable and can potentially lead
      to negative outcomes.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "volatile" with "violent."
    explanation: Although the words sound similar, they have different meanings. "Volatile"
      refers to rapid and unpredictable changes, while "violent" refers to extreme
      force or aggression.
