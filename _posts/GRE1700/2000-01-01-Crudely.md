    origin: The word "crudely" is derived from the adjective "crude," which originated

      with its meaning evolving over time to indicate a lack of refinement or polish.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Crude oil"'

      before being processed into usable products like gasoline, diesel, or plastics.

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "crudely" with "crudeness" or "crude" as an adjective.

      and "crude" is an adjective describing something in a raw or unrefined state.
