data:
  word: Industrious
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - Characterized by hard work and diligence.
    - Diligent in making an effort; conscientious.
    synonyms:
    - Hardworking
    - Diligent
    - Assiduous
    - Productive
    antonyms:
    - Lazy
    - Idle
    - Slothful
    - Unproductive
    examples:
    - She is an industrious worker and always completes her tasks on time.
    - The industrious student studied diligently for the exam and received a high
      grade.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of an industry where people work hard and diligently to produce goods
      or services.
    - '"Industrious" sounds like "industry" which is associated with hard work.'
  etymology:
    origin: Middle English, from Old French 'industrieus', from Latin 'industria'
      (diligence, industry)
    historical_usage: The word has been in use since Middle English and has maintained
      its meaning of diligent and hard-working throughout history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"An industrious bee"'
    explanation: Refers to someone who is diligently and constantly working, like
      a busy bee. This phrase emphasizes the continuous and productive nature of someone's
      work.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Mistaking "industrious" for simply being busy or occupied.
    explanation: Being busy or occupied does not necessarily imply being diligent
      or hard-working. Industriousness goes beyond mere activity and encompasses dedicated
      effort towards achieving goals.
