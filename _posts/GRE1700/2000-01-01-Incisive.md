    origin: 'Late Middle English (in the sense ''vengeful, corrosive''): from Old
      French, or from Latin incisivus, from incidere ''cut into''.'
    historical_usage: The word has evolved from its original meaning of being vengeful
      or corrosive to its current usage indicating intelligence and clarity.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Incisive wit"'
    explanation: Refers to someone who possesses sharp and clever humor.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing incisive with decisive.
