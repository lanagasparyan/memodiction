    origin: The word "boorish" originated from the Old English word "būr" which meant
      "farmer" or "peasant". The suffix "-ish" was added to the word to indicate the
      characteristic of being like a boor.
    historical_usage: The term "boorish" has been in usage since the 16th century,
      initially referring to someone from a rural or lower-class background lacking
      refined manners. Over time, it came to describe anyone who displays coarse and
      uncouth behavior, regardless of their background.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Boorish behavior"'

  - misconception: Confusing "boorish" with "boastful" or "brash".
