  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To remove or eliminate a group of people or things, often abruptly or violently.
    synonyms:
    - Expel
    - Remove
    - Eliminate
    antonyms:
    - Include
    - Retain
    examples:
    - The company began purging employees who were involved in the scandal.
    - The government purged all dissenting voices from the media.
    - The cleanse program aims to purge the body of toxins.
    mnemonics:
    - Imagine forcefully pushing something out, like squeezing a tube of toothpaste

  idioms:
