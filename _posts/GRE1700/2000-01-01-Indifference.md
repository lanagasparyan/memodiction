    - Apathy

    - Detachment
    antonyms:
    - Interest

    examples:

    - '"In the difference" - Indifference signifies a lack of difference or distinction,

  etymology:
    origin: From the Latin word "indifferentia," meaning "not making a difference"
      or "lack of difference."
    historical_usage: The term has been in use since the 15th century to describe
      a state of detachment or lack of interest.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"With indifference"'
    explanation: This phrase is used to describe the manner in which something is
      done without any strong emotions or interest.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing indifference with neutrality.
    explanation: While neutrality implies a lack of bias or taking sides, indifference

      engagement and empathy, whereas indifference suggests a disengagement or lack
      of empathy.
