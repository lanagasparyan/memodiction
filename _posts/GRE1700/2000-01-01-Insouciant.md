    origin: The word "insouciant" originated in the late 18th century from the French
      word "insouciant," which means "carefree" or "unconcerned."
    historical_usage: Although it is originally a French word, it has been adopted
      into the English language and has been used since the late 18th century to describe
      a lack of concern or indifference.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Insouciantly confident"'
    explanation: This phrase describes being confident without any sense of worry

  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "insouciant" with "nonchalant."
