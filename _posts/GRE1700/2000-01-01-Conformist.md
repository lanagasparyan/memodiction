  word: Conformist

  - part_of_speech: Noun

    synonyms:
    - Traditionalist
    - Follower
    - Conformer
    antonyms:
    - Nonconformist
    - Rebel
    - Maverick
    examples:
    - She always dressed according to the latest fashion trends, she was a true conformist.
    - The group of conformists followed the rules without questioning them.
    mnemonics:
    - '"Conformist" sounds similar to "conform," emphasizing the act of conforming
      to societal norms and expectations.'
    - Imagine a person standing in a line, perfectly aligning with everyone else,
      representing conformity.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "conformist" originated in the mid-19th century, derived from
      the verb "conform" and the suffix "-ist," which signifies a person who follows
      a particular practice, belief, or ideology.
    historical_usage: Though the term was coined relatively recently, the concept
      of conforming to societal norms and expectations has existed throughout history.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Sheep-like conformist"'
    explanation: This phrase implies blind adherence to established practices without
      questioning or individual thought, likening the conformist to a sheep that follows
      the herd without thinking for itself.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Conformists are always mindless followers.
    explanation: While some conformists may conform due to a lack of critical thinking
      or fear of standing out, others may conform consciously or choose to adhere
      to accepted practices for various reasons like maintaining social harmony or
      practicality. Conformity does not always indicate a lack of individuality or
      intelligence.
