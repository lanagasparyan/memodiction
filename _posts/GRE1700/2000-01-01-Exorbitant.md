    - Extortionate

    origin: The word "exorbitant" comes from the Latin word "exorbitans," present

      of those boundaries.
    historical_usage: The word "exorbitant" has been used in English since the late
      15th century to describe excessive or unreasonably high prices or amounts.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Exorbitant demands"'

  misconceptions:
