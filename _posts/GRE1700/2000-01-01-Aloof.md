data:
  word: Aloof
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Adjective
    definitions:
    - not friendly or forthcoming; cool and distant
    synonyms:
    - Distant
    - Detached
    - Remote
    antonyms:
    - Friendly
    - Approachable
    - Warm
    examples:
    - She kept an aloof attitude throughout the party and did not socialize much.
    - The boss remained aloof from the rest of the team, rarely interacting with them.
    mnemonics:
    - Think of someone standing alone at a party, looking aloof.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "aloof" originated from the Middle English word "a-loof" which
      meant "at a distance." It combines the Old English word "on" meaning "on" or
      "in" and "lóf" which means "windward" or "onto the wind."
    historical_usage: The use of "aloof" to mean "cool and distant" can be traced
      back to the 16th century.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Keep aloof"'
    explanation: To deliberately maintain a distance or detachment from someone or
      something.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "aloof" with "rude" or "arrogant."
    explanation: While aloofness can come across as unfriendly or unapproachable,
      it does not necessarily imply rudeness or arrogance. A person can be aloof without
      intentionally being rude.
