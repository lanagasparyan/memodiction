    origin: The word 'quack' originated from the Dutch word 'quacksalver' (a person

      or boast) and 'salve' (ointment).
    historical_usage: The term 'quack' has been used since the late 17th century to
      describe dishonest or fraudulent medical practitioners.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Quackery"'
    explanation: Refers to the practice or methods of a quack, usually associated

  regional_variations: []
