  word: Rebuff
  definitions:
  - part_of_speech: Verb
    definitions:
    - To reject, refuse, or dismiss someone or their offer, request, or suggestion
      in an abrupt or unkind manner.
    synonyms:
    - Snub
    - Reject
    - Spurn
    - Repulse
    antonyms:
    - Accept
    - Welcome
    - Embrace
    examples:
    - She rebuffed his advances and walked away.
    - The company rebuffed the investor's proposal.
    mnemonics:
    - Imagine someone pushing away a "buff" joke, rejecting it with a scowl.
    - Think of a "rebuff" as a "rejection" with emphasis.
  etymology:
    origin: The word "rebuff" originated in the mid-16th century and comes from the
      Old French word "rebuffier," which means to reject sharply or drive back.
    historical_usage: The term has been used consistently throughout history to depict
      the act of rejecting or repulsing someone or something.
  idioms:
  - expression: '"Meet with a rebuff"'
    explanation: Referring to an encounter or request being met with rejection or
      dismissal.
  regional_variations: []
  misconceptions:
  - misconception: Confusing "rebuff" with "refurbish."
    explanation: While the two words may sound similar, they have completely different
      meanings. "Rebuff" means to reject or dismiss, whereas "refurbish" means to
      renovate or repair something.
