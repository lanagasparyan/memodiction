## Word: Aberration

1. **Part of Speech**: Noun
   - **Definition(s)**
     - Definition 1: A departure or deviation from what is considered usual, normal, or expected.
   - **Synonyms & Antonyms**
     - Synonyms: anomaly, deviation, irregularity
     - Antonyms: conformity, norm, regularity
   - **Examples in Sentences**
     - Sentence 1: The sudden change in direction was an aberration from the usual route.
     - Sentence 2: The behavior she exhibited was an aberration compared to her usual calm demeanor.
   - **Mnemonics for Remembering**
     - Tip 1: Think of "a bizarre alteration" as a mnemonic for remembering the meaning of aberration.

2. **Etymology & History**
   - Origin: Late Middle English, from Latin 'aberratio', from aberrare meaning 'to stray'.
   - Historical Usage: The word has been used since the late Middle English period and has retained its meaning throughout history.

3. **Idiomatic Expressions/Phrases**
   - Expression 1: "Aberration of the mind"
   - Explanation: Refers to a mental disorder or a departure from rational thinking.

4. **Regional Variations (if applicable)**
   - [Not significantly varied across regions]

5. **Common Misconceptions/Errors**
   - Misconception/Error 1: Assuming aberration is always negative or wrong.
   - Explanation: While aberration can often refer to something unexpected or deviant, it does not necessarily imply negativity or incorrectness. It simply indicates a departure from the norm or what is typically expected.
